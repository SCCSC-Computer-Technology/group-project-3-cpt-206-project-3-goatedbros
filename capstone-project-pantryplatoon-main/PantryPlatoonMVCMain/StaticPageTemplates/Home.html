Welcome to the Chaser Food Pantry

Supporting students, faculty, and staff in times of need.

Who We Serve

The Chaser Food Pantry is dedicated to helping Spartanburg Community College students, faculty, and staff who are experiencing food insecurity. Whether it’s a short-term need or ongoing support, we are here to help.

How It Works

Individuals can make donations through our website or by visiting the pantry in person. We ensure all donations go directly to supporting those in our SCC community.

Other Pantry Services

Our team can assist students or refer them to community resources with the following needs:

  • Housing
  • Transportation
  • Benefit Applications – SNAP, TANF, FEMA Assistance
  • Utilities and Financial Counseling
  • ...and more

Accepted Donations

  • Non-perishable food items (canned goods, boxed meals, rice, etc.)
  • Household items (laundry detergent, dish soap, toilet paper, paper towel, cleaning supplies, trash bags)
  • Toiletries (soap, shampoo, toothpaste, feminine hygiene products)
  • Baby supplies (formula, baby food, diapers)

Donation Hours

Donations can be dropped off during regular campus hours. If you have a large donation, please contact us to coordinate.
